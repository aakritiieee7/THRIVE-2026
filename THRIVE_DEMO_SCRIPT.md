# THRIVE — Demo Script (for a live walkthrough or voiceover re-record)

This is the human-readable script behind the finished video. For the exact spoken
text, see `THRIVE_VOICEOVER.md`; for timings, see `THRIVE_DEMO_SHOTLIST.md`.

**Format:** 2 min 50 s. Ten beats. One sentence of setup → one clear claim → move on.
**Tone:** calm, teacher-first. Show; don't sell. Where the product refuses something,
say so — that is the proof it is not faking it.

---

### Beat 1 — Title (0:00)

> *"THRIVE. A teacher's daily companion, built for India's classrooms."*

**On screen:** title card.

---

### Beat 2 — What THRIVE is (0:07)

> *"This is THRIVE, the Teacher Hub for Resources, Instruction, Verified Information and Empowerment. It is built for teachers working in India's government and low-fee schools, where one teacher often handles several grade groups at once. THRIVE is built around that reality."*

**Show:** landing page with the five pillars.

**Why this matters:** the product is designed around multi-grade classrooms, which is
the norm in small government and low-fee schools.

---

### Beat 3 — The day, on one page (0:27)

> *"When a teacher signs in, everything needed for the day sits on one calm page. Saved plans, training progress, and the latest updates from the education department, each linked back to an official source."*

**Show:** today view.

---

### Beat 4 — Multi-Grade Classroom Compiler (0:41) — the flagship

> *"The heart of THRIVE is the Multi-Grade Classroom Compiler. A teacher says which grades are in the room, for how long, and what the children should learn. THRIVE builds one coordinated period. A shared opening, a dedicated teacher-led turn for each grade, independent work for the groups who are waiting, and the minutes always add up exactly. And if the plan is not possible in the time available, THRIVE says so, and explains why, instead of hiding it."*

**Show:** a generated plan; point at the minute total; then a deliberately infeasible
input and the explicit refusal message.

**Why this matters:** this is a deterministic engine, not a chat response. It cannot
silently overrun the period, and it cannot invent a schedule that does not fit.

---

### Beat 5 — Guided setup, and edits that stay (1:14)

> *"When a teacher is not sure where to start, guided setup asks a few simple questions and prepares the plan. Any part of it can be changed, and every section can be regenerated on its own, so a teacher's own edits are never lost."*

**Show:** guided questions → plan; edit one turn's activity; regenerate that section.

---

### Beat 6 — Teaching Assistant with visible sources (1:30)

> *"Then there is the Teaching Assistant. A teacher can ask, in English or in Hindi, how to explain evaporation using nothing but chalk and a blackboard. The answer is built from THRIVE's curriculum corpus, and every reply shows its sources, so it can be checked. When the corpus does not cover something, THRIVE says so, rather than making it up."*

**Show:** English question → answer + sources; switch to Hindi; ask something outside
the corpus and show the honest out-of-corpus reply.

---

### Beat 7 — Training Centre (1:55)

> *"The Training Centre holds short practice modules written by THRIVE. Progress is saved, and each module links to verified professional development from DIKSHA and NISHTHA. It is a learning record, not an official certificate."*

**Show:** modules list with progress and the linked DIKSHA/NISHTHA resources.

---

### Beat 8 — Verified updates (2:12)

> *"Verified Education Updates collects policy news and official resources, each with its source, with search and bookmarks. No unverified circulars, and no invented announcements."*

**Show:** updates with source links.

---

### Beat 9 — Teacher community (2:26)

> *"And the Teacher Community lets teachers share what actually works in the classroom, with replies, helpful votes, and moderation."*

**Show:** a discussion thread.

---

### Beat 10 — WhatsApp, and close (2:35)

> *"There is one more way in. Teachers can message THRIVE on WhatsApp, and get the same sourced answers, right from a phone. THRIVE. Helping teachers thrive, so students flourish."*

**Show:** `/demo` QR page, or the WhatsApp conversation.

---

## Things to say out loud (and not forget)

- Coverage is **Grades 1–5 core chapters in Mathematics and EVS, aligned to NCERT
  structure** — illustrative seed data, not validated syllabus coverage for every
  board or state.
- The assistant is grounded and cites sources; when it does not know, it says so.
- No student data is collected.
- No partnership or endorsement is claimed with NCERT, DIKSHA, NISHTHA, the Ministry of
  Education, SCERTs or the Azim Premji Foundation.

## Things not to say

- Any number of users, schools, teachers or students using the product.
- Any measured learning improvement.
- That it covers every Indian state or board.
- That the training modules are official certifications.
